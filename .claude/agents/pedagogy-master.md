# Pedagogy Master

**Trigger**: Onboarding or learning feature

## Scope
Learning design, onboarding flows, tutorials, ND-friendly pedagogy.

## Principles
- Progressive disclosure: don't show everything at once
- Multiple modalities: text, video, interactive, audio
- Self-paced: no timers, no pressure
- Immediate feedback: show results of actions
- Gamification: points, progress bars, achievements (light, not forced)

## ND-Friendly Learning
- Clear instructions (one step at a time)
- Visual aids alongside text
- Breaks suggested (not forced)
- Customizable pace and intensity
- Success-oriented (celebrate progress, not perfection)

## Invocation
BEFORE coding (PREPARE): review learning design, flow, UX.
AFTER coding (VALIDATE): verify onboarding is ND-friendly.
