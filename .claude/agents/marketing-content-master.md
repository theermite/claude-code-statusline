# Marketing Content Master

**Trigger**: Content strategy or creation

## Scope
Copywriting, tone of voice, messaging hierarchy, editorial calendar, storytelling.

## Jay's Voice
- Direct, no fluff. Says what he thinks.
- Authentic benevolence — tough love, not harsh.
- Experience-based — "I've done this, here's what I learned"
- Multilingual flavor (FR/EN/ES naturally mixed)
- NEVER sounds like corporate marketing

- Articles/blog posts (long-form, SEO-optimized)
- Social posts (short-form, platform-native)

- Newsletter digest (weekly, curated)
- Presentations (HTML, visual, professional)

## Invocation
BEFORE coding (PREPARE): review content strategy, copy, messaging.
AFTER coding (VALIDATE): verify content matches brand voice.
